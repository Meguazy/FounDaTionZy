# AD-NNN: Decision title

- Decision status: proposed
- Implementation status: planned
- Date: unset
- Owner / decision authority: unset
- Decide by: unset
- Related requirements / quality scenarios: unset
- Related ODs / experiments / step: unset
- Supersedes / superseded by: none

## Context and forces

Explain the problem, constraints, quality goals, evidence, and why a consequential
choice is needed. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.

## Options

Describe credible alternatives, including the simplest viable approach.
Explain their tradeoffs against this project's requirements.

## Proposed decision and rationale

State the selected recommendation and why it best fits the current constraints.
It remains proposed until the authorized decision is recorded.

## Consequences

List benefits, costs, risks, rejected capabilities, operational effects, and
conditions that might justify revisiting the decision.

## Acceptance or resolution

Record the actual decision, date, authority, rationale, scope, and any limits.
Keep this pending until the direction is given. A merged file is not acceptance.

## Implementation and evidence

Link code/configuration, checks, revision/tree, environment, outcomes, and limits
as they become available. Accepted direction alone is not implementation proof.
